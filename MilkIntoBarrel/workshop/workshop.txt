title=Milk Into Barrels
description=A small add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3436537035]Useful Barrels[/url]: milk your animals straight into a barrel!
description=
description=[b]This is my very first mod[/b] - I'm not a developer, I put this together for fun and for my own server. Please be kind, and constructive feedback is very welcome. :)
description=
description=[h1]What it does[/h1]
description=Stand next to a milkable animal with an open [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3436537035]Useful Barrels[/url] barrel nearby and milk it: the milk goes into your bucket and is [b]automatically poured into the barrel[/b]. You gain normal Husbandry XP, exactly like regular milking.
description=
description=Three ways to trigger it:
description=[list]
description=[*] Right-click the animal
description=[*] Right-click the barrel
description=[*] Radial menu (V key) near the animal
description=[/list]
description=
description=Works with [b]any milkable animal[/b] (cows, sheep... and animals added by other mods). The milk type matches the animal.
description=
description=[b]You need a bucket[/b] (any container that can hold the milk) and an open barrel (empty, or already holding the same milk) within ~3 tiles.
description=
description=[h1]Sandbox options[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Allow milking without a bucket (hybrid)[/b] - off by default. When ON you can milk with or without a bucket: WITH a bucket you get the normal Husbandry XP; WITHOUT a bucket the milk still goes straight into the barrel (with the milking animation) but grants no XP.
description=[*] [b]Milking duration multiplier (no-bucket only)[/b] - speed of the no-bucket milking (default tuned to the normal milking pace; higher = slower; no effect when you use a bucket).
description=[/list]
description=
